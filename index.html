Agate Lens Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

Agate Lens is a tool for scoring backlit stones and keeping a field journal. It works entirely on your device. There is no account, no server, and no analytics in this version.

What the app accesses

  • Camera. Used to show a live preview and to measure translucency, banding and colour. Images are processed on the device.
  • Photos you save. A find photo is stored in the app's private storage on your device. It is not uploaded.
  • Location (optional). Only if you grant location permission, coordinates are attached to a find and stored locally. You can use the app without granting it.
  • Flashlight. Used only when you tap the light button.
  • Internet. Used only when you tap a social link or the share button, which opens your system browser or share sheet.

Sharing

Sharing is always initiated by you. The app hands the selected photo, scorecard image or text to your device's share sheet. Agate Lens does not post anything on your behalf and does not receive data back from social apps.

Who receives your data

No one, as part of normal app operation. Your finds stay on your device. If you choose to share a find, the app in the share sheet you pick receives only what you selected.

Retention and deletion

Finds remain on your device until you delete them inside the app. You can also clear the app's data through Android settings. The kit checklist is stored locally and can be reset from the Kit tab.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If a future version adds cloud sync or analytics, this policy will be updated before that feature ships.
